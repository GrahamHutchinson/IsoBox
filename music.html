Music

Tracks you can download and try in IsoBox. Tap play to listen here, or Download to save a file.

Getting a track into IsoBox

  1. Download the file on your iPhone (it goes to the Files app, in Downloads).
  2. In Files, long-press the file, choose Move, and pick On My iPhone ▸ IsoBox.
  3. Open IsoBox. It finds the song within a moment, or pull to refresh the Library.
These tracks are shared for you to listen to and practise with. Please don’t re-upload or sell them.
