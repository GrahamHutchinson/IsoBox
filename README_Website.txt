IsoBox website: how to use
==========================
1. Edit site-config.js: your TestFlight link, your email, your privacy-policy address and your list of tracks.
2. Put your screenshots in "images", using the SAME file names as the placeholders (shot-library.png, shot-lyrics.png, ...).
   Portrait phone screenshots work best (any size; they are shown about 200 px wide).
3. Put your music in "music" and list each file in site-config.js.
4. Upload everything to a GitHub repository (this folder's contents, not the folder itself) and turn on
   Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch (main, root). The address will be https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPO/
5. Open the address on your phone and check it. To update later, just upload the changed files.
Notes: GitHub allows files up to 100 MB (about 1 GB per repository). Only share music you have the right to share.
