Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to email. Please include your phone model, iOS version and what you were doing.

Frequently asked

Does IsoBox include any music?

No. You bring your own files. The Music page has a few tracks to try.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Splitting songs, finding chords and timing lyrics all run on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

How long does splitting a song take?

A few minutes per song. Keep the screen on until it finishes. The result is stored inside the app.

Why can’t I import some songs from the Music app?

Only songs stored on your phone can be copied. Streaming-only songs are protected and can’t be imported.

The chords are wrong in places.

Chord detection is a good guess. Use Edit Detected Chords to fix them, or type the chords you know the song uses and listen again. Setting “Remove chords shorter than” higher gives a simpler chart.

How do I back up my library?

Settings ▸ Backup & Restore makes one file with everything in it.

How do I report a bug in the beta?

In TestFlight, open IsoBox and choose Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot inside the app and choose Share Beta Feedback.
